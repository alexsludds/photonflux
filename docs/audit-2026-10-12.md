# Codebase audit — 12 October 2026 (second pass)

Branch `claude/lucid-dijkstra-nl7lz5`, cut from `origin/main` at `3e33abb`
(#96). A follow-up to [`audit-2026-10.md`](audit-2026-10.md), covering what
landed since (#90–#96) and the open items that audit left.

Scope: `photonflux/`, `webapp/` (backend and frontend), `models/*.va`,
`examples/`, `tests/`. Physics first, then performance, then the browser UI.

**Environment.** A Linux x86-64 cloud container (4 cores, 16 GB):
`circulax 0.2.4`, the pinned bosdi#34 build, the CI-built Linux `openvaf-ir`,
`libngspice` 42 and the pinned SKY130 PDK. **stateye could not be installed**,
so everything that scores TDEC/TDECQ (`test_tdec.py`, `test_eyemeasure.py`,
the Eye tab's Measure button, the TDECQ steps of the example 48/49 flows) is
untested here.

**Baseline** (unmodified `3e33abb`, one pytest process per file): every test
passes except `test_fiber_nl.py`, which the kernel killed at 11 GB of RSS
after 440 s (see *Performance*), and the two stateye files, which skip.

## Fixed

Each physics or behaviour fix ships a test that fails on `3e33abb`.

### Physics and measurement

| Problem | Effect | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **Two optical sign conventions** (open item 1 of the last audit). circulax's waveguides, the Padé transit stages, the PN shifters, `fiber_cd`/`fiber_nl`, `ssfm.py` and `waveguide_nl.va` use the engineering carrier `e^{+jωt}`; the CW-laser WDM offset, every CMT ring (`ring_mod` and its parts, `ring_filter`, `ring_nl`, `ring_kerr`, `ring_selfheat`, `ring_mod_inj`, `cx.ring_phase_shifter`), the SOA/EDFA α_H phase, the EDFA gain-peak offset, `opt_filter`, `birefringent_wg.va` and the optical-spectrum display used the physics `e^{-iωt}`. | Invisible inside either family, wrong wherever they met: a WDM tone landed at its mirror frequency in a delayed-waveguide ring, a blue channel arrived *last* on positive-D fibre, chirp × dispersion had the wrong sign for rings and SOAs, and a birefringent arm rotated against a plain waveguide arm in a wavelength sweep. Everything now uses `e^{+jωt}`. For a self-consistent circuit the change is an exact conjugation, so the shipped examples are unchanged up to a relabelling of the lasers' arbitrary `phase` settings (checked trace by trace, see *Verification*). | `test_convention.py` (2 of 4 pins fail on the old code; the others guard the ring and the display) |
| **Directed Verilog-A inputs were open circuits.** Every coherent VA model (the `ring_mod` family, `soa`, `edfa`, `ring_comb`, `ring_nl`/`ring_kerr`/`ring_selfheat`, `wg_nl`, `wmirror`, the TW laser slices, `raman_amp`, `sbs_fiber`) read its input through `cx.field_to_ri`, a zero-current tap. A reciprocal circulax element in front of it (waveguide, splitter, MZM, coupler) therefore saw an open port and reflected, and the CW laser, an ideal source, reflected the wave back. The circulator already used the matched adapter `_field_to_ri_matched`, whose docstring describes exactly this. | The input read a standing wave instead of the transmitted field. One rule follows: one input per node, so a terminator or photodiode no longer belongs on an input's node. | Photodiode/APD shot noise was added **after** the transit-time poles. | Shot noise stayed white out to the noise-bank bandwidth whatever `f3db` said; with `f3db = 5 GHz` it was ~17 dB too strong at 20 GHz. Identical when `f3db = 0` (the default). | `test_apd.py::test_shot_noise_is_band_limited_by_the_detector` |
| Link report: the BER bathtub was plotted with the decision phase at −0.5 UI, and every stream phase used one symbol lag, so the half of the phases more than ½ UI from the decision sample were compared with the **neighbouring** symbol. The decision phase itself was the correlation peak, which can sit at an eye edge. | The bathtub showed a BER ≈ 0.5 shelf and its floor at the plot edge; BER/Q were reported at a sub-optimal phase. The bathtub now uses absolute sample indexing (±½ UI around the decision sample) and the report samples at the centre of the bathtub floor, as a BERT does. | `test_linkpost.py::test_link_report_samples_at_the_eye_centre` |
| Optimizer `eye:` objective scored every eye as NRZ, and took the UI from top-level sources only, ignoring the global baud rate and subcircuits. | A PAM-4 eye was scored on the gap between its middle clusters; a run whose UI comes from the top-bar baud got the 100 ps default. | — (two-line fix through `simulate.with_global_ui`) |

## Performance

All numbers on the 4-core container, cold JAX caches unless noted.

| Change | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `fiber_nl`: the split-step chain ran as an unrolled Python loop, and circulax differentiates a component with one JVP per unknown, so the compiled program grew with the **square** of the segment count. Now one `lax.scan`. | 20 segments (default): 40 s, 2.0 GB. 30: 88 s, 3.9 GB. 60: ~15 GB, OOM (it killed `test_fiber_nl.py`). | 12.9 s, 0.68 GB. 20 s, 0.73 GB. 21 s, 0.98 GB. Identical output (1e-16). |
| Waveguide with real transit (Padé chain), same restructuring. | 3 mm guide (64 stages per direction): 49 s, 1.5 GB | 24 s, 0.76 GB |
| `build_models` re-lowered all 21 Verilog-A models (AST rewrite + exec) and rebuilt every built-in class on each models-cache miss — each new waveform, SKY130 geometry, noise bank or LTI fit, i.e. most optimizer and sweep points — and the first run lowered all of them whatever the circuit used. Built-ins are now built once per process, only the ones a netlist uses, and `cx.va` memoises its loaded modules by content hash. The circuit and models caches key on the VA sources' mtimes, so an edited model is picked up on the next run without a restart. (The rest of a first run's ~6 s "models ready" is `import circulax`.) | 0.7 s per miss; ~1 s extra at start-up | ~0 per miss |
| AC/noise linearisation ran un-jitted (op-by-op dispatch). Now one `filter_jit` per structure. | EO AC sweep (example 42_mzm_tw_eo_bw): 18 s cold, 1.35 s warm | 4.2 s cold, 0.03 s warm |

## Recommendations (not done here)

1. **TW-laser gain filter.** `tw_seg`/`tw_gain_seg` advance each slice with
   `τ_s dR/dt + R = R_left`, a first-order low-pass of |H|² = 1/(1+(ωτ_s)²).
   Over the ~80 slice passes of a 400 µm cavity round trip that is a ~145 GHz
   wide gain filter centred on the frame, much narrower than a real gain
   bandwidth, so it biases mode competition and SMSR. The box (Preissmann)
   scheme `τ_s/2 (Ṙ + Ṙ_left) + R − R_left = dz/2 [L(R) + L(R_left)]` has exact
   magnitude for a pure delay, but it is non-dissipative: the laser then needs
   an explicit gain-bandwidth model, and the DFB/DBR/FP examples need
   re-validating. Worth doing as its own change.
2. **Compile time of large structures.** The same `lax.scan` restructuring
   applies to the traveling-wave PN shifter (`_pn_phase_shifter_tw`), whose
   ladder and transit chains are unrolled per section; it matters for long
   electrodes (16+ sections with real transit).
3. **CI**: run `pytest` in the built image, one process per file — a single
   process accumulates every compiled program (the baseline run reached 12 GB
   before the OOM killer).
4. Junction nonlinearity in `ring_phase_shifter` (use the abrupt-junction law
   `pn_phase_shifter` already has), one PRBS LFSR, and the remaining
   structural items of the last audit.
5. **Laser back-reflections.** `cw_laser` is an ideal source, so every wave
   returning to it reflects with Γ = −1: a 50 dB terminator or an
   unterminated splitter port forms a weak cavity with the laser, and its
   phase depends on path lengths to a fraction of a wavelength. An
   isolated (matched) source option would match how links are built and
   remove a class of length-sensitive artefacts.
6. `CLAUDE.md` describes a different project (Linear issues, `bun run
   build`); there is no JS build here — `node --check webapp/static/*.js`
   plus `pytest` is the equivalent gate.

## Verification

Everything below ran on the container described at the top, with the
pinned toolchain.

**Tests.** One pytest process per file on the final tree: 45 files, **328
passed, 0 failed**. Four tests skip because stateye is missing: the whole of
`test_tdec.py` and `test_eyemeasure.py`, and two TDECQ cases in
`test_flow.py`. Each new test fails on `3e33abb`: `test_convention.py` (3
of 4; the fourth guards the CMT ring), `test_ac_noise.py` (f_T),
`test_apd.py` (shot-noise bandwidth), `test_linkpost.py` (eye centre),
`test_coherent.py` (scale; FSE spike, 3 of 6), `test_directed_inputs.py` (all 4),
`test_live_structures.py` (VA edit).
`node --check` passes on every file in `webapp/static/`.

**Shipped webapp examples, old vs new, trace by trace.** I ran all 51 on
`3e33abb` and on this branch and compared every trace and extra plot:

- 39 are identical (max relative difference ≤ 1e-8; most are bit-for-bit).
  24 and 42_mzm_tw_eo_bw move by 1e-6 and 3e-8 (the AC matrices' 1 nS
  leak), and 39 and 40 by < 1e-4.
- **06 and 07 (SKY130 f_T) now run**; they failed on the old code.
- **18, 19 and 43 (WDM through splitters)** differ before the input-tap fix
  only by a relabelling of the arbitrary laser phases. circulax's splitter
  has `j` on one port, which the conjugation flips, so the lasers' relative
  phase changes and with it the inter-channel beat pattern. Channel means
  and spreads were unchanged. The proof: running 19 with every laser phase
  negated, plus π on the laser behind the `j` port, reproduces the old
  traces to **2e-9** after start-up. In 43 the same mapping left a 1%
  residual. That residual led to the next finding: a parasitic cavity
  through the open SOA input, whose round-trip phase includes the VOA's
  static phase.
- **The input-tap fix** (directed VA inputs matched) changes only 18, 19
  and 43, and there every optical power drops by exactly 4×: the doubled
  field is gone. Everything else moves by < 1e-4.
  Example 43's notes quote an operating point, so its lasers go from 15 to
  60 µW. That reproduces the old SOA output in all three documented
  scenarios: amp_out 1.27 / 0.675 / 0.0155 mW, against 1.29 / 0.679 /
  0.0154 mW before (default, `p_sat = 1e-3`, VOA at 20 dB/cm). 18 and 19
  quote no levels. They keep their 4 / 8 mW lasers, so their eyes are now
  4× smaller in photocurrent.
- **37 (Vernier laser from ASE)** is identical up to the 6 ns heater step.
  After it, the new mode grows out of the ASE floor sooner (8–10 ns mean
  2.6 vs 1.6 mW): in the new convention that mode sits at the mirrored
  envelope frequency, so different noise samples seed it. Both runs end in
  the same state, 5.3 vs 5.1 mW with OSA lines at 1310.00 and 1310.17 nm.
- **44 (APD receiver)** moves by < 1e-4 (`f3db = 0`). The shot noise now
  passes through two algebraic unknowns, which reorders the arithmetic at
  solver tolerance.
- **48 (CMR flow)**: the resonance step is identical; the eye step needs
  stateye on both trees. **49** runs a 60-iteration optimizer and hit the
  40-minute cap on both trees.
